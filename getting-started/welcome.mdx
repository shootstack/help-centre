Shootstack helps professional photographers deliver galleries, share photos with clients, and manage their studio workflow.

This help center is being built out. More guides will be added here soon.

## What you can do in Shootstack

- Create **projects** to organize a shoot or client job
- Upload photos to **media folders**
- Build **galleries** and share them with clients
- Manage **contacts** and client communication

## Next steps

Sign in to the [Shootstack app](https://app.shootstack.com) to get started.

If you need assistance, email [support@shootstack.com](mailto:support@shootstack.com).
