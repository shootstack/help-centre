When a piece of shoot work is finished, mark the task complete so your open list stays current. You can also look back at completed work when you need it.

## Mark a task complete

Turn on the **Completed** checkbox on the task row. Or open the **⋯** menu or right-click the row, then click **Mark as complete**.

To return the task to your open list, turn the checkbox off or click **Mark as incomplete**.

<Note>
You can have up to 500 open tasks. To mark a finished task incomplete again, complete or delete another open task first.
</Note>

## Show open or completed tasks

On **Tasks**, click **Open tasks** to see finished work. Click **Completed tasks** to return to what's still open.

On a project or contact, the **Tasks** tab shows **Open** and **Completed** in one table.

To narrow the list by date or change the sort, see [Filter and sort tasks](/guides/tasks/filter).

## How open tasks are grouped

On the open list, tasks appear under **Today**, **Upcoming**, and **No due date**. Overdue tasks stay under **Today**.

A past due date shows how many days ago it was due. A date in the next 7 days shows how many days are left; a later date shows the calendar date. Completed tasks appear in one list on **Tasks**.
