A task is one piece of work for a shoot or a client, such as a follow-up or a delivery. Keep it on your list until the work is done.

Open **Tasks** in the workspace menu to see every task in your workspace.

## Create a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tasks">
    In the workspace menu, click **Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new task">
    Click **New**. If the workspace has no tasks yet, click **Create task** instead.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Tasks page with New highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/tasks/overview-create-1.png" alt="Tasks page header with the New button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/tasks/overview-create-1-dark.png" alt="Tasks page header with the New button" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the work">
    In **What needs to be done?**, describe the task.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Create task dialog with description
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/tasks/overview-create-2.png" alt="Create task dialog with What needs to be done placeholder" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/tasks/overview-create-2-dark.png" alt="Create task dialog with What needs to be done placeholder" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date">
    Click **Set due date**, then pick a date or choose **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or **Next week**. You can't choose a date that has already passed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the task">
    Click **Create task**. The task appears on the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While creating, you can also click **Select a project** or **Select a contact** to attach the task right away.

<Note>
A workspace holds up to 5000 tasks, and up to 500 of them can be open at once. Finish or delete tasks when you need room for another.
</Note>

## Open a task

Click a task row to open **Update task**. You can also open the **⋯** menu on the row, or right-click the row, and click **Open**.

## Link a project or contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task">
    Click the task row, or open the **⋯** menu and click **Open**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a project or contact">
    Click **Select a project** or **Select a contact**, then pick one. You can clear the due date, project, or contact if you no longer need them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update task**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a task

<Warning>
Deleting a task removes it from your workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task menu">
    On **Tasks**, open the **⋯** menu on the row, or right-click the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">

## On a project or contact

A project's **Tasks** tab shows its tasks. A contact's **Tasks** tab does the same. Click **New task** in either tab's toolbar to add one.

The project or contact is already selected, and you can still change or clear it. See [Projects](/guides/projects/overview) and [Contacts](/guides/contacts/overview).
