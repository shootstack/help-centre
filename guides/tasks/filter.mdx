A long task list is hard to scan. Filter and sort **Tasks** to show only work from this week, this month, or a longer stretch.

Open **Tasks** in the workspace menu to filter and sort every task. On a project or contact, the **Tasks** tab lists the tasks for that project or contact.

## Filter tasks

For example, show only tasks due **This week**, or ones you created **This month**. To switch between open and completed tasks, see [Complete a task](/guides/tasks/complete).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter">
    On **Tasks**, click **Filter** in the toolbar.

    Choose a date field and a period:

    - Date field: **Due date**, **Created at**, or **Updated at**
    - Period: **This week**, **This month**, **This quarter**, or **This year**

    The table shows only matching tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filter">
    Click **Clear filters** to see every task again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sort tasks

A new workspace sorts by **Due date** in **Ascending** order, so upcoming work comes first. To change it, click **Sorted by** in the toolbar. Choose a field:

- **Due date**
- **Last updated**
- **Creation date**

Then pick **Ascending** or **Descending**.
