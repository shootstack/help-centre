A project holds one shoot, from the first upload to the gallery your client receives. Everything for the shoot lives inside it: media folders, galleries, gallery shares, tasks, and notes.

Open **Projects** in the workspace menu to see every project in your workspace. Each card shows its cover, status, and how many folders and galleries it has.

## Create a project

Start a project as soon as a shoot is booked, so you have a place for tasks and notes before the photos arrive.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    In the workspace menu, click **Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    Click **New**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/overview-create-1.png" alt="Projects page header with the New button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/overview-create-1-dark.png" alt="Projects page header with the New button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the project">
    In **Name**, enter the project name, for example the client's name and the date.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/overview-create-2.png" alt="Create project dialog with the Name field" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/overview-create-2-dark.png" alt="Create project dialog with the Name field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the project">
    Click **Create project**. Shootstack opens the new project on its **Content** tab, ready for your first media folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A workspace holds up to 1000 projects. Delete one you no longer need before creating another.
</Note>

## Set the cover photo

The cover is the photo on the project card, so pick one that helps you spot the shoot at a glance.

Open the project. At the top of the **Content** sidebar, click **Choose photo**. If the project has no cover yet, you can also drop a photo onto **Upload cover photo**.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/overview-cover-1.png" alt="Project Content sidebar with the Choose photo button" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/overview-cover-1-dark.png" alt="Project Content sidebar with the Choose photo button" />
</Frame>

If the card crops out the part you care about, open the card menu on **Projects** and click **Change focal point**.

Drag the photo until that part shows; the change saves as you drag. Click **Done** to close.

## Search projects

With many projects, search is faster than scrolling. On **Projects**, click the search icon in the header or press **/**, then type at least two characters of the name.

## Delete a project

<Warning>
Deleting a project also deletes all its photos and galleries. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>

  </Step>

## Inside a project

A project has five tabs, and most of the work happens in the first one.

- **Content**: the media folders and galleries for this shoot. This is where you upload photos.
- **Gallery shares**: the links you have sent to clients.
- **Tasks**: to-dos for this shoot.
- **Notes**: notes for this shoot.
- **Activity**: what happened in this project, and when.
