Favorites keep the shoots you're working on right now in the workspace sidebar, under **Favorite projects**. Add a project when you start on it, and remove it once the shoot is delivered.

## Add a project to favorites

On **Projects**, open the project card's **⋯** menu. Click **Add to favorites**.

Or, hover the project name and click the star in the card that opens. Or, inside the project, click the star next to its name.

<Note>
You can favorite up to 10 projects. When you hit the limit, remove a delivered shoot to make room.
</Note>

## Open a favorite project

In the workspace sidebar, click the project under **Favorite projects**. The group appears once you have at least one favorite.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/favorites-open-1.webp" alt="Workspace sidebar showing the Favorite projects group" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/projects/favorites-open-1-dark.webp" alt="Workspace sidebar showing the Favorite projects group" />
</Frame>

## Remove a project from favorites

Once a shoot is delivered, take it off the list. Any of these works:

- In the project card menu, click **Remove from favorites**.
- Click the filled star next to the project name.
- In the sidebar, hover over the project under **Favorite projects** and click **Remove from favorites**.

<Tip>
To see only favorites on **Projects**, click **Filter**. Set **Is favorite** to **Yes**.
</Tip>
