Set how a gallery looks before anyone else sees it. Every change saves as you make it, and the preview updates with each one.

## Open design settings

On a large screen, **Design settings** is already open beside the preview. On a smaller screen, click **Design settings** on the gallery toolbar to open the same controls from the side.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-open-1.webp" alt="Gallery toolbar with Design settings, and the design panel beside the preview" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-open-1-dark.webp" alt="Gallery toolbar with Design settings, and the design panel beside the preview" />
</Frame>

The panel opens on the **Gallery** tab. The **Cover** tab sets the first screen your clients see. See [Set the cover](/guides/galleries/cover).

## Choose a theme, font, and color palette

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a theme">
    Open **Theme**. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**. **System** matches the light or dark setting on the device that opens the gallery. New galleries start on **Light**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-theme-1.webp" alt="Design settings on the Gallery tab, with Theme, Font, and Color palette" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-theme-1-dark.webp" alt="Design settings on the Gallery tab, with Theme, Font, and Color palette" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font">
    Open **Font** and pick one, or type in **Search font...** to find it. You can choose from Noto Sans, Geist, Antonio, Instrument Serif, Marcellus, Spectral, Amatic SC, Oooh Baby, and Ms Madi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a color palette">
    Open **Color palette** and pick one, or type in **Search color palettes...** to find it. Your own palettes show up next to Neutral, Slate, Taupe, Mauve, Mist, and Olive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the photo grid

A new gallery starts with the first layout, photo size **M**, and spacing 10.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a layout">
    Under **Grid layout**, click one of the three unlabeled icons. The first stacks photos in columns and keeps each photo's shape. The second lines them up in rows of similar height. The third puts each photo in an equal square.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-grid-1.webp" alt="Grid layout as three icons, with Photo size S, M, and L, and the Photo spacing slider" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-grid-1-dark.webp" alt="Grid layout as three icons, with Photo size S, M, and L, and the Photo spacing slider" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the photo size">
    Open **Photo size**. Choose **S**, **M**, or **L**. **S** fits more photos on the screen, and **L** makes each photo larger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the spacing">
    Drag **Photo spacing** to change the gap between photos, from 0 to 100.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Write an introduction

Add a short note clients will read with the photos.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the introduction">
    Click **Intro**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the note">
    Type in **Introduction text**. It shows in the gallery, centered above the photos.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-intro-1.webp" alt="Design settings on the Intro tab, with the Introduction text field" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/design-intro-1-dark.webp" alt="Design settings on the Intro tab, with the Introduction text field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
