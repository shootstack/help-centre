A gallery shows your client photos from a shoot. Choose the photos from media folders, then create a gallery share to send them.

## Where galleries live

Galleries belong to a project. Open the project and go to the **Content** tab. Click **Galleries** in the left sidebar to see each gallery as a card.

## Create a gallery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Galleries">
    On the project's **Content** tab, click **Galleries** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new gallery">
    Click **New**. If the project has no galleries yet, click **Create gallery** instead. **New** is in the header; you can also use the plus on the **Galleries** row in the sidebar. On a small screen, **New** is a plus icon.

<Note>
A project holds up to 25 galleries. Delete one you no longer need before you create another.
</Note>

## Open a gallery

Open a gallery to check how it will look before you send it.

Click the gallery card, or click the gallery in the left sidebar. You can also right-click the gallery and choose **Open**.

Shootstack opens a live preview of the gallery. Click **View preview** to open it in a new tab. The preview isn't the link you send your client.

## Rename a gallery

Right-click the gallery card, or hover the gallery in the sidebar and click **⋯**. You can also click **⋯** on the card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Rename">
    In the gallery menu, click **Rename**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new name">
    Type the new name, then click **Rename gallery to** to confirm.

## Delete a gallery

<Warning>
Deleting a gallery also stops its gallery shares from working. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the gallery menu">
    Right-click the gallery card, or hover the gallery in the sidebar and click **⋯**. You can also click **⋯** on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete gallery**, click **Delete**.

## Inside a gallery

The open gallery is where you choose the photos and how they look.

- [Add media folders](/guides/galleries/folders): choose which media folders appear in the gallery.
- [Design a gallery](/guides/galleries/design): set the theme, font, colors, and photo grid.
- [Set the cover](/guides/galleries/cover): choose the first thing your client sees.

Once your folders, design, and cover are in place, click **Share gallery** to start a gallery share for your client. The photos still live in a media folder. See [Media folders](/guides/media-folders/overview).
