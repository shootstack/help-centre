Choose the photo and text your clients see when they open the gallery. Open **Design settings** on the gallery toolbar.

## Choose a cover template

The template sets the layout, and it decides whether you get a title, a subtitle, and extra covers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Cover tab">
    In **Design settings**, click **Cover**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-template-1.webp" alt="Design settings with the Cover tab selected" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-template-1-dark.webp" alt="Design settings with the Cover tab selected" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the template list">
    Click **Cover template**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-template-2.webp" alt="Cover template menu listing Pure, Signature, Editorial, Artist, Contour, Muse, and Stories" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-template-2-dark.webp" alt="Cover template menu listing Pure, Signature, Editorial, Artist, Contour, Muse, and Stories" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Click Pure, Signature, Editorial, Artist, Contour, Muse, or Stories.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the title and subtitle

Not every template has a title or subtitle, so a missing field is normal. Pure has neither. Artist has only a **Title**. Signature, Editorial, Contour, Muse, and Stories have both.

Type the main line in **Title** and, if your template has one, the line under it in **Subtitle**. Shootstack saves your changes after a moment.

## Choose the cover photo

You pick the cover photo from the photos in the gallery's media folders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the photo list">
    Under **Cover photos**, click **Select cover**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-photo-1.webp" alt="Cover photos section with the Select cover button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-photo-1-dark.webp" alt="Cover photos section with the Select cover button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a photo">
    In **Select cover**, click the photo. The list stays open, so you can try another before you close it.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-photo-2.webp" alt="Select cover panel with photos from the gallery" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/galleries/cover-photo-2-dark.webp" alt="Select cover panel with photos from the gallery" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the list is empty, the gallery has no media folder yet. See [Add media folders](/guides/galleries/folders).

## Set the focal point

Move the focal point when the crop hides a face or the part of the photo you want clients to see.

Drag the round focal point handle on the cover photo. Shootstack saves the new position after a moment.

Once an extra cover has a photo, drag its handle the same way.

## Add extra covers

Only Stories has extra covers, and you fill them in order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the next extra cover">
    Click **Select cover** on the next Extra cover row. Each row unlocks once the row above it has a photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the photo">
    In **Select cover**, click the photo. The list stays open after you pick one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Choose Stories when you need more than one cover photo. It has 5 extra covers; the other templates have one cover photo.
</Note>
