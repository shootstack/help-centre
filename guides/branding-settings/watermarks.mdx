<Note>
A workspace holds up to 3 custom watermarks. Built-in watermarks don't count. At the limit, edit an existing watermark instead.
</Note>

## Upload the watermark image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the watermark menu">
    Right-click the watermark row, or click **⋯** on it.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Custom watermark row menu with Edit highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/watermarks-upload-1.png" alt="Watermark row menu with Edit visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/watermarks-upload-1-dark.png" alt="Watermark row menu with Edit visible" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the watermark">
    Click **Edit**.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Update watermark dialog with Upload watermark highlighted

  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Under **Watermark image**, click **Upload watermark**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your image">
    Choose a JPG/JPEG, PNG, or WebP file from your computer. It uploads and saves separately from the watermark's other settings.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Update watermark dialog showing the uploaded image
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/watermarks-upload-3.png" alt="Update watermark dialog with an uploaded watermark image" />

    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Each original image can be up to 5 MB. For a larger file, export a smaller copy before uploading.
</Note>

<Note>
Each original image can be up to 2048 pixels wide or high. Resize a larger image before uploading.
</Note>

## Use a watermark in a media folder

Choose the watermark when creating or duplicating a media folder. See [Media folders](/guides/media-folders/overview).

## Edit or delete a watermark

To change a custom watermark later, choose **Edit** again. Built-in watermarks can't be edited or deleted.

<Warning>
Deleting a watermark also removes its image from your workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the watermark menu">
    Right-click the watermark row, or click **⋯** on it.
