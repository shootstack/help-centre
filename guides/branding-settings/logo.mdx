Your logo helps clients recognize your photography business in shared galleries. Upload versions for light and dark backgrounds so the logo stays readable.

## Upload a logo for light backgrounds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open branding settings">
    Click **Branding settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the logo tab">
    Click **Logo** to choose which version to upload.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Branding settings with Logo and both logo cards visible
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-open-2.png" alt="Branding settings with the Logo tab and two logo cards" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-open-2-dark.png" alt="Branding settings with the Logo tab and two logo cards" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    In the **Logo** card, click **Upload logo**.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Logo card with Upload logo highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-light-1.png" alt="Logo card with the Upload logo button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-light-1-dark.png" alt="Logo card with the Upload logo button" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your logo">
    Choose a JPG/JPEG, PNG, or WebP file from your computer. Shootstack uploads it right away and shows it in the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use a logo with a transparent background for the best results.

<Note>
Each original image can be up to 5 MB. For a larger file, export a smaller copy before uploading.
</Note>

<Note>
Each original image can be up to 2048 pixels wide or high. Resize a larger image before uploading.
</Note>

## Upload a logo for dark backgrounds

Use a version that stays readable on dark backgrounds and photos, such as a white logo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    In the **Logo for dark backgrounds** card, click **Upload logo**.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Logo for dark backgrounds with Upload logo highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-dark-1.png" alt="Logo for dark backgrounds card with the Upload logo button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/logo-dark-1-dark.png" alt="Logo for dark backgrounds card with the Upload logo button" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dark-background version">
    Choose the file from your computer. Shootstack uploads it right away and shows it in the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your logo doesn't appear, check the gallery design. Some cover designs don't show a logo.

## Replace a logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the logo to replace">
    Click **Upload logo** in the card for the version you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the replacement">
    Choose the new file from your computer. It replaces the logo in that card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a logo

<Warning>
Deleting a logo removes that version from your workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
