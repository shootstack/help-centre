Social media links give clients a way to find more of your work from a shared gallery. Add the profiles you use for your photography business.

## Add or change a social media link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/social-media-add-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/social-media-add-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open branding settings">
    Click **Branding settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your social media settings">
    Click **Social media**. Your profile fields appear under **Your social media**.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Social media tab with Your social media fields visible
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/social-media-add-2.png" alt="Social media tab with the seven profile fields" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/social-media-add-2-dark.png" alt="Social media tab with the seven profile fields" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your profile">
    In the field for your platform, enter your username or paste the full profile URL. To change an existing link, replace the text in the field.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Social media settings with a username entered in Instagram
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/social-media-add-3.png" alt="Social media settings with a username in the Instagram field" />

    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the link">
    Press **Enter**. Clicking outside the field also saves it, and Shootstack turns a username into a profile URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add links for:

- **Facebook** and **Instagram**
- **X (Twitter)** and **TikTok**
- **YouTube** and **LinkedIn**
- **Pinterest**

For **YouTube**, enter your channel handle or the full channel URL. In **X (Twitter)**, you can paste a Twitter profile URL. Shootstack saves it as an x.com link.

## Remove a social media link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear the profile field">
    Delete the field's contents, or click the clear icon at its right edge.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Instagram field with the clear icon highlighted
    <Frame>

    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the empty field">
    Press **Enter**, or click outside the field. The saved link is removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where clients see your links

Clients see social media icons in gallery footers, including download and password screens. Only platforms with a saved link appear. Clicking an icon opens the profile in a new tab.

The gallery preview shows the icons, but they aren't clickable there. Open a gallery share to check your links.
