## Update your business contact details

Add the details you use for your business, and leave any others empty.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    In **Website**, enter your business or portfolio website URL. You can paste a full link or enter the domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your email address">
    In **Email**, enter the address you use for business inquiries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your phone number">
    In **Phone**, enter your business phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save each change">
    After editing a field, click outside it or press **Enter**. Each change saves separately.

## Clear a contact detail

Clear a detail when you no longer use it, such as an old business phone number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear the field">
    Use the clear icon at the end of the field, or delete its text.

  <Step title="Save the empty field">
