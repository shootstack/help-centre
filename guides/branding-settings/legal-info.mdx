Legal links let clients read your policies from a shared gallery. Add the web addresses of policy pages you've already published.

## Add or change a legal link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-1.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-1-dark.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open branding settings">
    Click **Branding settings**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-2.webp" alt="Branding settings page with the Legal info tab visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-2-dark.webp" alt="Branding settings page with the Legal info tab visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your legal settings">
    Click **Legal info** to add or change links under **Your legal info**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-3.webp" alt="Legal info tab with Terms of service, Privacy policy, and Cookie policy fields" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-3-dark.webp" alt="Legal info tab with Terms of service, Privacy policy, and Cookie policy fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the policy link">
    Paste the full web address into the field for the policy page. To change a link, type over its current address.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-4.webp" alt="Legal info settings with a full URL in the Privacy policy field" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-add-4-dark.webp" alt="Legal info settings with a full URL in the Privacy policy field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the link">
    Press **Enter**, or click outside the field. Each change saves separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the field that matches your page:

- **Terms of service** for your terms page.
- **Privacy policy** for your privacy policy page.
- **Cookie policy** for your cookie policy page.

Each field is optional. Leave any you don't use empty.

## Remove a legal link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear the policy field">
    Delete the web address from the field you want to clear.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-remove-1.webp" alt="Legal info settings with the Privacy policy field cleared" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-remove-1-dark.webp" alt="Legal info settings with the Privacy policy field cleared" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the empty field">
    Press **Enter**, or click outside the field. The saved link is removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where clients see your links

Clients see your policy links in gallery footers, including download and password screens. Only policies with a saved link appear. Clicking a link opens the policy page in a new browser tab.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-links-1.webp" alt="Shared gallery footer with Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookie Policy links" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/legal-info-links-1-dark.webp" alt="Shared gallery footer with Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookie Policy links" />
</Frame>

The gallery preview shows the links, but they aren't clickable there. Open a gallery share to check them.
