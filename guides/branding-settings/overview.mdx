Branding settings are where you prepare the logos and colors for your shared galleries, along with watermarks for your photos. You can also keep your business details here.

## Open branding settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/overview-open-1.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/overview-open-1-dark.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Branding settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open branding settings">
    Click **Branding settings**. Shootstack opens **Logo**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/overview-open-2.webp" alt="Branding settings page with Logo selected and both logo cards visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/branding-settings/overview-open-2-dark.webp" alt="Branding settings page with Logo selected and both logo cards visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're already in settings, click **Branding** under **Workspace** in the sidebar.

## Find the setting you need

Choose a tab at the top of **Branding settings**:

- **Logo**: [upload logos for light and dark backgrounds](/guides/branding-settings/logo).
- **Color palettes**: [create reusable colors for your galleries](/guides/branding-settings/color-palettes).
- **Watermarks**: [create image watermarks for your media folders](/guides/branding-settings/watermarks).
- **Contact info**: [keep your business email, phone number, and website up to date](/guides/branding-settings/contact-info).
- **Social media**: [add social profile links to gallery footers](/guides/branding-settings/social-media).
- **Legal info**: [link to your existing policy pages from gallery footers](/guides/branding-settings/legal-info).

Choose a palette in your gallery's design settings, or a watermark when you create a media folder. The linked guides below cover those steps.

## Return to your workspace

Click **Settings** beside the back arrow at the top of the sidebar to return to your workspace's home page.
