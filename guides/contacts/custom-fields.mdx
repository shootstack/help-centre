Custom fields keep extra information about your clients alongside their contact details. Create a field once for your workspace, then fill it in on each contact.

## Open field settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click **Workspace settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open contact fields">
    Under **Workspace**, expand **Custom fields**. Click **Contact**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-open-1.webp" alt="Contact fields settings with Contact selected in the sidebar and Create field visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-open-1-dark.webp" alt="Contact fields settings with Contact selected in the sidebar and Create field visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new field">
    Click **Create field**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the field type">
    Choose a **Type** for the information you keep. Use **Select** for one choice or **Select multiple** for several.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-create-1.webp" alt="Create custom field dialog showing the field types" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-create-1-dark.webp" alt="Create custom field dialog showing the field types" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the field">
    In **Name**, enter a label for the information, such as a client's preferred location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add choices if needed">
    For choice fields, click **Add options...**. Pick a color, type a choice name, then click **Add** for each choice.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-create-2.webp" alt="Custom field options popover showing the color picker, choice name, and Add button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-create-2-dark.webp" alt="Custom field options popover showing the color picker, choice name, and Add button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the field">
    Click **Create custom field**. The new field appears on your contacts and as a column in the contact list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A saved field's type can't be changed. To fill in its values, see [Edit a contact](/guides/contacts/details#fill-in-custom-fields).

<Note>
A workspace holds up to 25 custom fields for contacts. If you reach the limit, delete a field you no longer need before adding another.
</Note>

## Edit a field

<Warning>
Removing a choice clears it from contacts that use it. Other selections in a multiple-choice field stay. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field menu">
    Right-click the custom-field row, or hover it and click **⋯**. Choose **Update**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-edit-1.webp" alt="Custom field row menu showing Update and Delete" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-edit-1-dark.webp" alt="Custom field row menu showing Update and Delete" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the field">
    In **Edit custom field**, change **Name**. For a choice field, add or remove choices under **Options**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the changes">
    Click **Save changes**. If you removed existing choices, confirm with **Remove options** in the confirmation dialog.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-remove-1.webp" alt="Remove options confirmation showing the removed choices and affected contact count" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-remove-1-dark.webp" alt="Remove options confirmation showing the removed choices and affected contact count" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A choice field holds up to 25 options. Remove choices you no longer need before adding more.
</Note>

## Delete a field

<Warning>
Deleting a custom field also deletes its values from every contact in your workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field menu">
    Right-click the custom-field row, or hover it and click **⋯**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete custom field?**, click **Delete field**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-delete-1.webp" alt="Delete custom field confirmation showing the field name, affected contact count, and Delete field button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/custom-fields-delete-1-dark.webp" alt="Delete custom field confirmation showing the field name, affected contact count, and Delete field button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
