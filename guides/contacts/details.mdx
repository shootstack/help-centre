Keep a client's record current after a booking or whenever their details change. Every change in **Contact details** saves as you make it.

## Edit contact details

**Contact details** starts open beside the contact. If it is closed, click **Contact details** on the toolbar.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-edit-1.webp" alt="Open contact with the Contact details sidebar" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-edit-1-dark.webp" alt="Open contact with the Contact details sidebar" />
</Frame>

Or, from **Contacts**, right-click a row and choose **Edit contact**. It opens **Update contact** with the built-in contact fields.

Click **Email** or **Name** to update either field. You can change **Birthday** or **Phone** the same way. **Website** and **Company** are editable here too.

## Set status and source

Use **Status** and **Source** so you can find the right people later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a status">
    Open **Status** and pick a status:

    - **Prospect** or **Lead**
    - **Client** or **Partner**
    - **Lost** or **No status**
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-status-1.webp" alt="Contact details sidebar with the Status menu open" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-status-1-dark.webp" alt="Contact details sidebar with the Status menu open" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a source">
    Open **Source** and pick how you met them, such as:

    - **Referral**
    - **Event**
    - **Direct**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fill in custom fields

Keep extra client information in your workspace's custom fields. To add a field, see [Manage contact custom fields](/guides/contacts/custom-fields).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    On **Contacts**, click the contact row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the custom fields">
    If the panel is closed, click **Contact details** on the toolbar. In **Contact details**, find **Custom fields**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-custom-fields-1.webp" alt="Contact details sidebar showing custom fields and Set value controls" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-custom-fields-1-dark.webp" alt="Contact details sidebar showing custom fields and Set value controls" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a value">
    Click an existing value or **Set value...**, then enter a value or choose an option. For typed values, press **Enter** or click outside the field to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Dates and option selections save as you choose them. For a yes/no field, pick **Yes** or **No**.

To clear a value from this contact:

- Erase a typed value, then press **Enter**.
- For a single-choice or yes/no field, choose **No value**.
- For multiple choices, remove the selected options with their × buttons.
- For a date, use the calendar's clear button.

## Add an address

Under **Address**, enter the address in this order:

- **Address** and **Address 2**
- **City** and **Region**
- **Postal code** and **Country**

Under **Social media**, enter a username or URL.

## Change the profile photo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open photo options">
    Click the contact's avatar, then choose **Change photo**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-photo-1.webp" alt="Contact avatar menu with Change photo and Delete photo" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-photo-1-dark.webp" alt="Contact avatar menu with Change photo and Delete photo" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop and apply">
    In **Crop profile photo**, use **Zoom** to adjust the image. Then click **Apply** to update the avatar right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the photo, open the avatar menu and click **Delete photo**.

<Note>
Use a JPG, PNG, or WEBP file up to 100 MB. If yours is larger, choose a smaller file.
</Note>
