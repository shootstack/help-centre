Keep a client's record current after a booking or whenever their details change. Every change in **Contact details** saves as you make it.

## Edit contact details

**Contact details** starts open beside the contact. If it is closed, click **Contact details** on the toolbar.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-edit-1.png" alt="Open contact with the Contact details sidebar" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-edit-1-dark.png" alt="Open contact with the Contact details sidebar" />
</Frame>

From **Contacts**, right-click a row and choose **Edit contact**. It opens **Update contact** with the same fields.

Click **Email** or **Name** to update either field. You can change **Birthday** or **Phone** the same way. **Website** and **Company** are editable here too.

## Set status and source

Use **Status** and **Source** so you can find the right people later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a status">
    Open **Status**. Pick **Prospect**, **Lead**, or **Client**. You can also pick **Partner**, **Lost**, or **No status**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-status-1.png" alt="Contact details sidebar with the Status menu open" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-status-1-dark.png" alt="Contact details sidebar with the Status menu open" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a source">
    Open **Source** and pick how you met them, such as **Referral**, **Event**, or **Direct**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add an address

Under **Address**, enter **Address** and **Address 2**. Then fill in **City** and **Region**, followed by **Postal code** and **Country**.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-address-1.png" alt="Contact details sidebar with the Address section open" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-address-1-dark.png" alt="Contact details sidebar with the Address section open" />
</Frame>

## Add social links

Under **Social media**, enter a username or URL.

## Change the profile photo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open photo options">
    Click the contact's avatar, then choose **Change photo**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-photo-1.png" alt="Contact avatar menu with Change photo and Delete photo" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/details-photo-1-dark.png" alt="Contact avatar menu with Change photo and Delete photo" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop and apply">
    In **Crop profile photo**, use **Zoom** to adjust the image. Then click **Apply**. The avatar updates right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the photo, open the avatar menu and click **Delete photo**.

<Note>
Use a JPG, PNG, or WEBP file up to 100 MB. If yours is larger, choose a smaller file.
</Note>
