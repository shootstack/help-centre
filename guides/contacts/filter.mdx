A long contact list is hard to scan. Filter and sort **Contacts** to pull up the right people, and choose which columns show so you only see the fields you use.

## Filter contacts

For example, show only **Client** contacts, or people you contacted **This month**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter">
    On **Contacts**, click **Filter** in the toolbar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/filter-filter-1.webp" alt="Contacts toolbar with the Filter button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/filter-filter-1-dark.webp" alt="Contacts toolbar with the Filter button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to show">
    Choose **Status** or **Last contacted at**, then pick a value. The table shows only matching contacts.

    For other filters, add the matching column first:

    - **Favorite** or **Source**
    - **Country**
    - **Created at** or **Updated at**

    For dates, pick a period such as **This week** or **This month**.

    Custom fields with choices, yes/no values, or dates also appear here when their columns are visible. Choose the field name, then pick the values or date period to match.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/filter-custom-fields-1.webp" alt="Contacts Filter menu showing a custom field and its choices" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/filter-custom-fields-1-dark.webp" alt="Contacts Filter menu showing a custom field and its choices" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filter">
    Click **Clear filters** to see every contact again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you send a gallery share email, **Last contacted at** updates automatically.

## Sort contacts

A new workspace sorts by **Name** in **Ascending** order. To change it, click **Sorted by** in the toolbar and choose a built-in contact field:

- **Email** or **Name**
- **Company** or **Last contacted**
- **Last updated** or **Creation date**

Then pick **Ascending** or **Descending**. **Last contacted** helps you get back to whoever you emailed last.

## Choose columns

Click **Columns** in the toolbar to show or hide fields. The table starts with:

- **Name** and **Email**
- **Status** and **Phone**
- **Tasks** and **Notes**
- **Last contacted at**

Click **Add column** to bring in another field. If the list is long, type in **Search column...**.

New custom fields appear as columns automatically. If you hide one, use **Add column** to show it again. To create a field, see [Manage contact custom fields](/guides/contacts/custom-fields).

Drag a column by its handle to change its position, or click its × to hide it. Hiding a column keeps its values and any active filter; use **Clear filters** to show every contact again.
