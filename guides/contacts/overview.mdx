A contact is someone you work with on a shoot, such as a client, planner, or partner. Keep one for each client so you can find them when you send a gallery or follow up.

Open **Contacts** in the workspace menu to see every contact in your workspace.

## Create a contact

Add a contact when you book a shoot, so you have their email when you share galleries.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    In the workspace menu, click **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new contact">
    Click **New**. If the workspace has no contacts yet, click **Create contact** instead.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Contacts page with New highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/overview-create-1.png" alt="Contacts page header with the New button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/overview-create-1-dark.png" alt="Contacts page header with the New button" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name">
    In **Name**, enter the contact's name.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Create contact dialog with Name and Email
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/overview-create-2.png" alt="Create contact dialog with Name, Email, and Company fields" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/overview-create-2-dark.png" alt="Create contact dialog with Name, Email, and Company fields" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email">
    In **Email**, enter their email address. You can add a company under **Company (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the contact">
    Click **Create contact**. Shootstack opens the new contact on the **Activity** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A workspace holds up to 1000 contacts. Delete one you no longer need before creating another.
</Note>

## Open a contact

Click a contact row to open it on the **Activity** tab. You can also right-click the row and choose **Open contact**. Close the contact to get back to **Contacts**.

## Search contacts

With many contacts, search is faster than scrolling. On **Contacts**, click the search icon in the header or press **/**, then type at least two characters of the name, email, or company.

## Delete a contact

<Warning>
Deleting a contact also deletes its tasks, notes, and files. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact menu">
    On **Contacts**, right-click the contact row. Inside a contact, open the **⋯** menu next to the contact name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete contact**, click **Delete**.

## Inside a contact

Use the contact's five tabs to find what you need while working with a client:

- **Activity**: what happened with this contact, and when.
- **Emails**: gallery share emails sent to this contact.
- **Tasks**: to-dos for this contact.
- **Notes**: notes about this contact.
- **Files**: contracts and other files kept with this person. See [Add files to a contact](/guides/contacts/files).
