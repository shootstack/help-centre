Favorites keep the clients you're working with right now in the workspace sidebar, under **Favorite contacts**. Add a contact when a shoot is active, and remove it once the shoot is delivered.

## Add a contact to favorites

On **Contacts**, right-click a row and click **Add to favorites**.

You can also open the contact and click the star next to the name.

  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/favorites-add-1.png" alt="Contacts row menu with the Add to favorites option" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/favorites-add-1-dark.png" alt="Contacts row menu with the Add to favorites option" />
</Frame>

<Note>
You can favorite up to 10 contacts. When you hit the limit, remove a contact whose shoot is delivered to make room.
</Note>

## Open a favorite contact

In the workspace sidebar, click the contact under **Favorite contacts**. The group appears once you have at least one favorite.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/favorites-open-1.png" alt="Workspace sidebar showing the Favorite contacts group" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/contacts/favorites-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace sidebar showing the Favorite contacts group" />
</Frame>

## Remove a contact from favorites

Choose one of these ways to remove a favorite:

- Right-click the row and click **Remove from favorites**.
- Click the filled star next to the contact name.
- In the sidebar, hover over the contact under **Favorite contacts** and click **Remove from favorites**.

<Tip>
On **Contacts**, add the **Favorite** column. Then click **Filter** and choose **Favorite**. Set it to **Yes**.
</Tip>
