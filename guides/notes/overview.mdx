A note is where you write down shoot details, such as a shot list, a client request, or delivery notes. Link each one to a project or contact so you can find it when you need it.

Open **Notes** in the workspace menu to see every note in your workspace.

## Create a note

Jot down the details while the shoot is still fresh. You can link the note to a project or contact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notes">
    In the workspace menu, click **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new note">
    Click **New**. If the workspace has no notes yet, click **Create note** instead. A note card appears on the list.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Notes page with New highlighted
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/notes/overview-create-1.png" alt="Notes page header with the New button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/notes/overview-create-1-dark.png" alt="Notes page header with the New button" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A workspace holds up to 5000 notes. Delete a note you no longer need when you need room for another.
</Note>

## Open a note

Click a note card to open **Edit note**. Or click **⋯** on the card or right-click it, then choose **Open**.

## Name a note

In **Edit note**, click **Untitled note** and type a title. Shootstack saves as you type.

## Link a project or contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the note">
    Click the note card, or open the **⋯** menu and click **Open**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a project or contact">
    Click **Select a project** or **Select a contact**, then pick one. You can clear either if you no longer need it.

## Search notes

On **Notes**, click the search icon in the header or press **/**, then type at least two characters. A project's or contact's **Notes** tab has no search.

## Delete a note

<Warning>
Deleting a note removes it from your workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the note menu">
    On the **Notes** tab, open the **⋯** menu on the card, or right-click the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete note**, click **Delete**.

## On a project or contact

Linked notes also appear on a project's **Notes** tab or a contact's **Notes** tab. Click **New** there to add a note. On a narrow screen, only the plus icon appears.

The project or contact is already selected, and you can still change or clear it. See [Projects](/guides/projects/overview) and [Contacts](/guides/contacts/overview).
