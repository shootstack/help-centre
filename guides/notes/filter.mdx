When your note list grows, filter by date to find recent notes, or sort **Notes** to scan it.

Open **Notes** in the workspace menu to filter and sort every note. A project's or contact's **Notes** tab has no filter or sort.

## Filter notes

For example, show only notes you created **This week**, or ones you updated **This month**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter">
    On **Notes**, click **Filter** in the toolbar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/notes/filter-filter-1.png" alt="Notes toolbar with the Filter button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/notes/filter-filter-1-dark.png" alt="Notes toolbar with the Filter button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to show">
    Choose **Created at** or **Updated at**, then pick a period. Choose **This week** or **This month** for a shorter range, or **This quarter** or **This year** for a longer one. The list shows only matching notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filter">
    Click **Clear filters** to see every note again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sort notes

A new workspace sorts notes by **Last updated** in **Descending** order, so recent changes appear first. Click **Sorted by** in the toolbar to change it. Choose a field:

- **Title**
- **Last updated**
- **Creation date**

Then pick **Ascending** or **Descending**.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/notes/filter-sort-1.png" alt="Notes toolbar with the sort menu open" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/notes/filter-sort-1-dark.png" alt="Notes toolbar with the sort menu open" />

Note templates have their own sort. See [Note templates](/guides/notes/templates).
