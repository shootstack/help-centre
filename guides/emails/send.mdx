Email a gallery when you want your client to receive the link and your message together. You can create a share as you send, or email a share you've already created.

## Email a new gallery share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the gallery">
    On the project's **Content** tab, open the gallery you want to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the email">
    Click **Share gallery**, then choose **Compose email**. The **Share gallery** sheet opens on **Email**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-new-1.webp" alt="Gallery toolbar Share gallery menu with Compose email" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-new-1-dark.webp" alt="Gallery toolbar Share gallery menu with Compose email" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address the email">
    In **To**, choose a contact or type an email address. Enter a **Subject**, then write your message below it.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-new-2.webp" alt="Share gallery sheet on Email with recipient, subject, and message fields" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-new-2-dark.webp" alt="Share gallery sheet on Email with recipient, subject, and message fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose share settings">
    Click **Next** to move through **Security** and **Favorites**. Continue through **Download** and **Review** to choose how your client opens and uses the gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the gallery">
    On **Overview**, click **Send email**. Shootstack creates the gallery share and its email, then opens the project's **Gallery shares** list.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-new-3.webp" alt="Share gallery sheet on Overview with the Send email button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-new-3-dark.webp" alt="Share gallery sheet on Overview with the Send email button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

For help with access and download choices, see [Security](/guides/gallery-shares/security) and [Downloads](/guides/gallery-shares/downloads).

## Email an existing gallery share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share's emails">
    From the project's **Gallery shares** list, open the share and click **Emails**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose another email">
    Click **Compose email** to open the **Compose gallery share email** dialog. If the email list is empty, use the **Compose email** button there.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-existing-1.webp" alt="Gallery share Emails tab with the Compose email button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-existing-1-dark.webp" alt="Gallery share Emails tab with the Compose email button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the email">
    Choose a contact or type an address in **To**, enter a **Subject**, and write your message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the email">
    Click **Send email**. The message appears in the share's email list.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-existing-2.webp" alt="Compose gallery share email dialog with recipient, subject, message, and Send email" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/send-existing-2-dark.webp" alt="Compose gallery share email dialog with recipient, subject, message, and Send email" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A gallery share holds up to 50 emails. To email more people, create another share for the gallery.
</Note>

## Write the message

The message editor shows a preview as you type. You can also [insert a saved template](/guides/emails/use-templates) into an empty message.

The body holds up to 3,000 text characters. Formatting can take up extra space. If the editor warns you about this, remove some formatting before sending.

If **Email sending paused** appears, contact [support@shootstack.com](mailto:support@shootstack.com) to restore access.

## What the recipient receives

Your email includes the subject, your message, and a link to the gallery. If you turned on **Password protected**, it also includes the gallery password. If you turned on **PIN protected**, it includes the download PIN.
