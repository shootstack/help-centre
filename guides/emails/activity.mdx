After you email a gallery share, check its latest status and what happened next. You can also review the message you sent.

## Read the status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open email history">
    Open **Emails** in the workspace menu, then choose **Gallery shares**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the message's status">
    Find the message by its recipient or subject. The **Status** column shows the latest recorded update.
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/activity-status-1.webp" alt="Gallery share email history with a status badge in the Status column" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/activity-status-1-dark.webp" alt="Gallery share email history with a status badge in the Status column" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Look for Delivered to confirm delivery. Waiting and Sent don't confirm it yet. A Blocked message can appear in the history even if it wasn't sent.

## Read email events

Click the message's row to open **Gallery share email**. Under **Email events**, you'll see each recorded status with its date and time. A message can have more than one event as its status changes.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/activity-events-1.webp" alt="Email events timeline showing status badges and their dates" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/activity-events-1-dark.webp" alt="Email events timeline showing status badges and their dates" />
</Frame>

If the timeline is empty, no activity has been recorded yet. Check again later if you're waiting for an update.

## Review the sent preview

Scroll to **Preview** to review the saved message. If the email included a gallery password or download PIN, you'll see it there too.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/activity-preview-1.webp" alt="Preview of a sent gallery share email below its event timeline" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/activity-preview-1-dark.webp" alt="Preview of a sent gallery share email below its event timeline" />
</Frame>

The View gallery button is part of the email preview; it doesn't open the gallery here.
