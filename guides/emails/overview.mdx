Emails keeps a record of the gallery share messages you send to contacts. Open a message to check its details and delivery activity.

## Where emails live

Open **Emails** in the workspace menu to see every gallery share email in your workspace. The **Gallery shares** tab lists them newest first, with the recipient, subject, gallery, status, and date.

You can also see messages for one gallery share on its **Emails** tab, or messages for one contact on the contact's **Emails** tab.

## Open a sent email

Click an email row to open **Gallery share email**. You'll see who it was addressed to, the gallery and subject, plus **Email events** and a **Preview** of the message.

## Where to compose

To email a new gallery share, start from an open gallery's **Share gallery** menu. To send another message for an existing share, open the share's **Emails** tab. See [Send a gallery share email](/guides/emails/send) for the steps.

## Where templates live

Open **Templates** beside **Gallery shares** to create and edit messages you can reuse when composing an email. See [Create email templates](/guides/emails/create-templates).
