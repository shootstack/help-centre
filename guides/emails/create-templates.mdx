Save a message you often send with gallery shares as a template. You can update it whenever the wording changes.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Open **Emails** in the workspace menu, then click **Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the template">
    Click **New**. If there are no templates yet, click **Create template** instead. A new card appears in the list, ready to edit.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-create-1.webp" alt="Templates tab with the New button above the template cards" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-create-1-dark.webp" alt="Templates tab in dark mode with the New button above the template cards" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open and edit a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template">
    Click its card to open **Edit template**. You can also right-click the card, or click **⋯** on it, then choose **Open**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the message">
    Type a title and write your message in the editor. Your changes save automatically; the footer shows when they're saved.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-edit-1.webp" alt="Edit template dialog with a title field, message editor, and save status" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-edit-1-dark.webp" alt="Edit template dialog in dark mode with a title field, message editor, and save status" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The editor holds up to 3,000 characters of text. If formatting takes too much space, simplify or remove it.

## Search and sort templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    Click the search icon in the **Templates** header.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-search-1.webp" alt="Templates header with the search icon highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-search-1-dark.webp" alt="Templates header in dark mode with the search icon highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the template">
    Type at least two characters from its title, then click a result to open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list starts with the newest templates first. To change the order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sorting">
    Click **Sorted by** above the cards.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-sort-1.webp" alt="Templates sort menu with Title, Last updated, Creation date, Ascending, and Descending" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-sort-1-dark.webp" alt="Templates sort menu in dark mode with the ordering choices" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sort by">
    Pick **Title**, **Last updated**, or **Creation date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the order">
    Pick **Ascending** or **Descending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a template

<Warning>
Deleting a template removes it from the workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template menu">
    Right-click the template card, or click **⋯** on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete email template**, click **Delete**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-delete-1.webp" alt="Delete email template dialog with the Delete button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/create-templates-delete-1-dark.webp" alt="Delete email template dialog in dark mode with the Delete button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
