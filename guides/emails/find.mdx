sidebarTitle: Find emails

---

To check a gallery share email's message or activity, open **Emails** in the workspace menu and choose **Gallery shares**.

## Search emails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    Click the search icon in the page header. Its tooltip says **Search gallery share emails**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-search-1.png" alt="Gallery shares tab with the search icon in the header" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-search-1-dark.png" alt="Gallery shares tab with the search icon in the header" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a search">
    Enter at least two characters from the recipient, subject, or gallery name.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-search-2.png" alt="Gallery share email search with subject, recipient, and gallery in the results" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-search-2-dark.png" alt="Gallery share email search with subject, recipient, and gallery in the results" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Click a matching result to see the email's details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Press **/** on **Gallery shares** to open search.
</Tip>

## Filter by status or sent date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open filters">
    On **Gallery shares**, click **Filter** in the toolbar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-filter-1.png" alt="Gallery shares toolbar with the Filter button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-filter-1-dark.png" alt="Gallery shares toolbar with the Filter button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose statuses">
    Under **Status**, pick one or more statuses to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sent date range">
    Under **Sent date**, pick **This week**, **This month**, **This quarter**, or **This year**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filters">
    Click **Clear filters** to show the full list again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sort the history

The list starts with the newest emails first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sorting">
    Click **Sorted by** in the toolbar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-sort-1.png" alt="Sorting menu with Sent date, Last updated, Ascending, and Descending" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/emails/find-sort-1-dark.png" alt="Sorting menu with Sent date, Last updated, Ascending, and Descending" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sort by">
    Pick **Sent date** or **Last updated**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the order">
    Pick **Ascending** or **Descending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
