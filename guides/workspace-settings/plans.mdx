Choose a plan when you're ready to start a subscription for your workspace. Compare storage for your photos and monthly allowances for downloads and shared gallery views before opening checkout.

## Compare plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/plans-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/plans-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click **Workspace settings**. Shootstack opens your workspace details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the plan comparison">
    Under **Workspace** in the settings sidebar, click **Plans**. The comparison shows the available plans and their allowances.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/plans-open-3.png" alt="Plans page with plan prices, storage allowances, and monthly usage limits" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/plans-open-3-dark.png" alt="Plans page with plan prices, storage allowances, and monthly usage limits" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the switch above the table to choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing. The plans shown change to match your choice.

Prices are displayed per month for comparison. For yearly billing, the displayed amount is a monthly equivalent. The label beneath each price tells you whether the plan is billed monthly or yearly.

Compare the allowances shown for your workspace:

- Photo storage is the total space for all the photos you upload.
- Contact file storage is the total space for files attached to contacts.
- Monthly download bandwidth covers the data used for photo, gallery, and contact file downloads.
- Monthly gallery views are page views across your shared galleries.

Feature checkmarks show which plans include galleries, contacts, contact files, and emails. Use the amounts shown in the comparison to choose the allowance that fits your uploads and gallery usage.

<Tip>
If you're on a free trial, click **Choose plan** on the **Billing** page's trial card to open the comparison.
</Tip>

## Start your subscription

Click **Choose plan** beneath the plan you want. Checkout opens outside Shootstack in the same tab, where you can continue setting up your subscription.

If your workspace already has a plan, the comparison buttons are disabled. Use **Manage plan** on **Billing** to manage your existing subscription. See [Manage your subscription](/guides/workspace-settings/billing).
