Workspace settings are where you manage the details shown to your contacts and the subscription for your photography business.

## Open workspace settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.

    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click **Workspace settings**. Shootstack opens **General** under **Workspace**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find the setting you need

Choose a page under **Workspace** in the settings sidebar:

- **General**: [change your workspace name and icon](/guides/workspace-settings/general).
- **Plans**: [compare plans and choose a subscription](/guides/workspace-settings/plans).
- **Billing**: [manage your subscription and billing](/guides/workspace-settings/billing) or [check your workspace usage](/guides/workspace-settings/usage).

General also shows your existing unique domain. It can't be changed.

Branding has its own settings for logos, colors, and watermarks. Your personal preferences are covered in [Personal settings](/guides/personal-settings/overview).

## Return to your workspace

Click **Settings** beside the back arrow at the top of the sidebar to return to your workspace's home page.
