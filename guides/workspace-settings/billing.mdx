Open **Billing** to check the dates for your trial or plan and manage your subscription or payment details.

## Review your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/billing-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/billing-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click **Workspace settings**. Shootstack opens your workspace details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Under **Workspace** in the settings sidebar, click **Billing**. Shootstack shows your current subscription information.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/billing-open-3.png" alt="Billing page with the subscription card and its dates visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/billing-open-3-dark.png" alt="Billing page with the subscription card and its dates visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're on a trial, the **Free trial** card shows the trial dates. Otherwise, review the plan name, current status, and dates shown on your subscription card.

## Open plan management

Click **Manage plan** on your subscription card to open the billing portal in the same tab.

If **Update payment** or **Pay invoice** appears, click the button to open the same billing portal and manage the payment.

If the card shows **Choose plan**, click it to open the plan comparison. See [Choose a plan](/guides/workspace-settings/plans).

If no plan is found, click **Contact support** to open support chat, or email [support@shootstack.com](mailto:support@shootstack.com).
