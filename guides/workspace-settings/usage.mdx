Check your workspace's usage before uploading a new shoot or sending galleries to clients. You'll see the storage used and this month's downloads and gallery views.

## Check and refresh usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/usage-open-1.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/usage-open-1-dark.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click **Workspace settings**. Shootstack opens your workspace details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Click **Billing** under **Workspace** in the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh your usage">
    Find **Current usage** and click **Refresh** to update the figures.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/usage-refresh-1.webp" alt="Current usage card with the Refresh button and usage figures" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/usage-refresh-1-dark.webp" alt="Current usage card with the Refresh button and usage figures" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The amounts shown apply to your current plan or trial. Compare the amount used with the limit beside it. Refreshing updates the figures; it doesn't free storage or reset monthly usage.

## Check your storage

Total photos counts the photos currently in your workspace. Photo storage is the space those photos use. Contact file storage covers files you've attached to contacts.

These storage totals carry over each month.

If your photo storage is full, free up space by [deleting photos you no longer need](/guides/media-folders/organize), or [upgrade your plan](/guides/workspace-settings/billing).

If your contact file storage is full, [delete files you no longer need](/guides/contacts/files) or change your plan.

## Check monthly gallery usage

Download bandwidth / month is the data used when you or your contacts download photos, galleries, or contact files.

Gallery views / month counts page views across your shared galleries. Repeat views count too, so this isn't the number of individual visitors.

You get a new download bandwidth and gallery view allowance at the start of each calendar month.

Compare the figures with the limits shown on your page. If you need more than your current plan allows, see [Manage your subscription](/guides/workspace-settings/billing).
