Your workspace name and icon appear in the app sidebar and at the top of shared galleries. Update them when your business name or logo changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/general-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/general-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Workspace settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Click **Workspace settings**. Shootstack opens your workspace details.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/general-open-2.png" alt="Workspace settings with the workspace name and icon controls" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/workspace-settings/general-open-2-dark.png" alt="Workspace settings with the workspace name and icon controls" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're already in settings, click **General** under **Workspace**.

## Change your workspace name

In **Name**, type over the current workspace name. Use the name your contacts recognize, such as your company name. Changes save after you stop typing.

## Upload or replace your workspace icon

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Click **Upload icon**. Use the same button to replace your current icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an image">
    Choose a JPG/JPEG, PNG, or WebP file from your computer. Shootstack opens **Crop icon**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the crop">
    Drag the image to position it in the square crop and adjust **Zoom** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the icon">
    Click **Apply**. The dialog closes and your new workspace icon appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Cancel** in the crop dialog to discard the selected image and keep your current icon.

<Note>
The original image can be up to 5 MB. For a larger file, export a smaller copy before uploading.
</Note>

<Note>
The original image can be up to 2048 pixels wide and tall. Resize a larger image before uploading, even if you plan to crop it in Shootstack.
</Note>

## Remove your workspace icon

<Warning>
Deleting your workspace icon removes it from your workspace. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove the icon">
    Click **Remove icon** beside your current icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete workspace icon**, click **Delete**. The first letter of your workspace name replaces the icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>
