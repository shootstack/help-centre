title: Set a password and expiry
sidebarTitle: Security

---

A password decides who can open a gallery share. An expiration date decides when it closes. You set both when you create the link, and you can change them on the share later. Leave both off to keep the gallery open to anyone with the link.

## Require a password

A new gallery share has no password, so anyone with the link can open it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security">
    On the gallery, click **Share gallery**, then **Create link**. **Share gallery** opens on **Security**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-password-1.webp" alt="Share gallery dialog on Security, with Password protected off" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-password-1-dark.webp" alt="Share gallery dialog on Security, with Password protected off" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on password protection">
    Turn on **Password protected**. Keep the password that appears, click **Generate** for a new one, or type your own. Then give it to your client.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-password-2.webp" alt="Password protected on, with a password filled in and Generate" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-password-2-dark.webp" alt="Password protected on, with a password filled in and Generate" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the link">
    Click **Next**. On **Overview**, check the password, then click **Create link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the password later, open the share from the project's **Gallery shares** list. On **Info**, open **Security settings**. You can change **Password protected** and **Password** there. Changes save right away.

Check **Access type** on the **Gallery shares** list to see whether a share is Password (protected) or Public (open).

## Set an expiration date

Without an expiration date, the gallery stays open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the expiration date">
    On **Security**, turn on **Expiration date**. On a share you already created, it's called **Expires** under **Security settings**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-expiry-1.webp" alt="Security step with the Expiration date switch" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-expiry-1-dark.webp" alt="Security step with the Expiration date switch" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the day">
    Choose the day the gallery should close. It closes when that day begins, so pick the day after the last day your clients should see the photos.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-expiry-2.webp" alt="Calendar open on Select an expiration date" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-expiry-2-dark.webp" alt="Calendar open on Select an expiration date" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the switch is on, pick a day. Without one, your clients can't open the link.

For a new link, click **Next**. On **Overview**, click **Create link**. On **Info**, **Expiration date** saves as you change it. Turning the switch off opens the gallery again.

## What the client sees

When the share has a password, your client lands on **Welcome to your gallery**. They type the password in **Gallery password** and click **Access gallery**.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-client-1.webp" alt="Welcome to your gallery with Gallery password and Access gallery" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-client-1-dark.webp" alt="Welcome to your gallery with Gallery password and Access gallery" />
</Frame>

From the expiration date on, your client sees **Gallery no longer available**.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-client-2.webp" alt="Gallery no longer available" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/security-client-2-dark.webp" alt="Gallery no longer available" />
</Frame>

A download PIN is separate from this password. See [Allow downloads](/guides/gallery-shares/downloads).
