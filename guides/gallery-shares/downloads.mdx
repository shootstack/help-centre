Choose what your clients can download from a gallery share, and see what they took. Set a PIN and limits on the share's **Info** tab, where changes save right away, or on the **Download** step when you create the link.

## Require a download PIN

A PIN keeps downloads locked until your clients enter it. They aren't asked for one until you turn on **PIN protected**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open download PIN settings">
    On **Info**, open **Download PIN settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on PIN protection">

## Allow a gallery download

A new share already lets clients download the whole gallery at once, with **Gallery download limit** set to 5.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open gallery download settings">
    On **Info**, open **Gallery download settings**.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Gallery download settings with the limit at 5
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-gallery-1.png" alt="Gallery download settings with Allow gallery download on, Gallery download limit at 5, and Original file and Web optimized file checked" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-gallery-1-dark.png" alt="Gallery download settings with Allow gallery download on, Gallery download limit at 5, and Original file and Web optimized file checked" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave gallery download on">
    Leave **Allow gallery download** on. Turn it off if clients should not take the whole gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the download limit">
    With **Gallery download limit** on, enter how many gallery downloads the share allows, from 1 to 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the files">
    Under **Gallery download files**, uncheck **Original file** or **Web optimized file** if clients shouldn't get that version. Both start checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A share allows up to 50 gallery downloads, even with **Gallery download limit** off. For fewer, leave it on and enter a lower number.
</Note>

## Allow photo downloads

A new share already lets clients download photos one at a time, with **Photo download limit** off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open photo download settings">
    On **Info**, open **Photo download settings**.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Photo download settings with the limit off
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-photo-1.png" alt="Photo download settings with Allow photo download on and Photo download limit off" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-photo-1-dark.png" alt="Photo download settings with Allow photo download on and Photo download limit off" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave photo download on">
    Leave **Allow photo download** on. Turn it off if clients should not save photos one at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a lower limit">
    Turn on **Photo download limit** and enter how many photo downloads the share allows, from 1 to 500. It starts at 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the files">
    Under **Photo download files**, uncheck **Original file** or **Web optimized file** if clients shouldn't get that version. Both start checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A share allows up to 500 photo downloads, even with **Photo download limit** off. Turn it on to set a lower number.
</Note>

A photo with a watermark can't be downloaded. See [Media folders](/guides/media-folders/overview).

## See download activity

Open **Gallery downloads** to see each time a client downloaded the whole gallery, and **Photo downloads** to see each single photo. Each row shows whether they took the original or the web version. A gallery download can take a moment to prepare.
