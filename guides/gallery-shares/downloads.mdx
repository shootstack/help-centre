Choose what your clients can download from a gallery share, and see what they took. Set a PIN and the limits on **Download** when you create the link.

## Require a download PIN

Clients aren't asked for a PIN until you turn on **PIN protected**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Download">
    On the gallery, click **Share gallery**, then **Create link**. Click **Next** until you reach **Download**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on PIN protection">
    Turn on **PIN protected**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-pin-1.png" alt="Download settings with PIN protected switched on" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-pin-1-dark.png" alt="Download settings with PIN protected switched on" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the PIN">
    Enter a 4-digit PIN in the boxes that appear. Clients type it in **Unlock downloads** before they download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Allow a gallery download

A new share already lets clients download the whole gallery at once, with **Gallery download limit** set to 5.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find gallery downloads">
    On **Download**, find **Allow gallery download**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-gallery-1.png" alt="Download settings with Allow gallery download on, Gallery download limit at 5, and Original file and Web optimized file checked" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-gallery-1-dark.png" alt="Download settings with Allow gallery download on, Gallery download limit at 5, and Original file and Web optimized file checked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave gallery download on">
    Leave **Allow gallery download** on. Turn it off if clients should not take the whole gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the download limit">
    With **Gallery download limit** on, enter how many gallery downloads the share allows, from 1 to 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the files">
    Under **Gallery download files**, uncheck **Original file** or **Web optimized file** if clients shouldn't get that version. Both start checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A share allows up to 50 gallery downloads, even with **Gallery download limit** off. For fewer, leave it on and enter a lower number.
</Note>

## Allow photo downloads

A new share already lets clients download photos one at a time, with **Photo download limit** off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find photo downloads">
    On **Download**, find **Allow photo download**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-photo-1.png" alt="Download settings with Allow photo download on and Photo download limit off" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/downloads-photo-1-dark.png" alt="Download settings with Allow photo download on and Photo download limit off" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave photo download on">
    Leave **Allow photo download** on. Turn it off if clients should not save photos one at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a lower limit">
    Turn on **Photo download limit** and enter how many photo downloads the share allows, from 1 to 500. It starts at 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the files">
    Under **Photo download files**, uncheck **Original file** or **Web optimized file** if clients shouldn't get that version. Both start checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A share allows up to 500 photo downloads, even with **Photo download limit** off. Turn it on to set a lower number.
</Note>

A photo with a watermark can't be downloaded. See [Media folders](/guides/media-folders/overview).

To change these later, open the share from the project's **Gallery shares** list. On **Info**, use **Download PIN settings** for the PIN. Use **Gallery download settings** or **Photo download settings** for what clients can take. Changes save right away.
