A gallery share is the link your client opens to see a gallery. Create one once your photos and design are in place, then send it to your client.

## Where gallery shares live

Gallery shares belong to a project. Open the project and click **Gallery shares** to see every share for the shoot, newest first.

You create a share from an open gallery, not from this list.

You can also start a share from **Compose email** on the gallery. Emailing gets its own section later.

<Note>
A project holds up to 50 gallery shares. Delete one you no longer need before you create another.
</Note>

<Note>
Your plan includes a set number of gallery views each month. Once your workspace uses them up, clients can't open your galleries until the first of next month. Upgrade your plan to raise the limit.
</Note>

## Open a gallery share

Click the row. You can also right-click the row, or hover it and click **⋯**, then choose **Open**.

The share opens on **Info**, under the heading **Gallery share** rather than its own name.

## Rename a gallery share

Rename a share so you can tell it apart on the list.

Right-click the row, or hover it and click **⋯**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Rename">
    In the row menu, click **Rename**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new name">
    In **Options**, type the new name, then click **Rename gallery share to** to confirm.

## Share the link

Click **Gallery share link** on the row, or on the **Info** tab, to copy it.

{/* TODO screenshot: Gallery shares table with Gallery share link highlighted

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/overview-link-1-dark.png" alt="Gallery shares table with the Gallery share link column" />
</Frame>
*/}

Turn **Visibility** off on the row, or on **Info**, when clients should no longer open the link.

To see the gallery the way your client does, click **Open live** in the row menu. It opens in a new tab. If **Visibility** is off, Shootstack asks to publish the share first. Click **Publish and open**.

## Delete a gallery share

<Warning>
Deleting a gallery share removes the link, so clients can't open it anymore. Everything collected on the share is deleted too. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Right-click the row, or hover it and click **⋯**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete gallery share**, click **Delete**.

## Inside a gallery share

- [Set a password and expiry](/guides/gallery-shares/security): who can open the link, and until when.
- [Collect favorites](/guides/gallery-shares/favorites): let clients mark favorite photos.
- [Allow downloads](/guides/gallery-shares/downloads): control downloads, and see what clients took.
- [Collect reviews](/guides/gallery-shares/reviews): let clients leave a review.

For the gallery itself, see [Galleries](/guides/galleries/overview). Deleting a gallery stops its shares from working. The photos stay in their [media folder](/guides/media-folders/overview) in the [project](/guides/projects/overview).
