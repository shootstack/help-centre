A review is feedback your client leaves on a gallery share. Turn reviews on to hear what they thought of the shoot, then read what they submit on the share.

## Allow reviews

A new share already lets clients leave a review after they look at the gallery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Review">
    On the gallery, click **Share gallery**, then **Create link**. Click **Next** until you reach **Review**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/reviews-allow-2.png" alt="Share gallery on Review, with Allow reviews switched on" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/reviews-allow-2-dark.png" alt="Share gallery on Review, with Allow reviews switched on" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave reviews on">
    Leave **Allow reviews** on, or turn it off if your client should only view the photos. The share keeps that choice when you click **Create link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change it later, open the share from the project's **Gallery shares** list. On **Info**, open **Review settings**. Changes save right away.

## Choose the questions

A new share asks every question. Clear the ones you don't need so the form fits the shoot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the questions">
    On **Review**, with **Allow reviews** on, the questions are listed underneath. They stay hidden while reviews are off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the questions you don't need">
    Clear the box next to any question you don't want to ask. The choices include **Overall experience**, **Communication**, **Photo session**, and **Price/quality**. You can also turn off **Photo quality**, **Title**, **Message**, or **Recommendation**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your choices save when you click **Create link**. On **Info**, the same boxes are grouped under **Review fields**, and each change saves right away.

## Read a review

Once a client sends a review, click **Reviews** on the gallery share. The tab shows how many have come in.

Each row is one review. You see who sent it under **Contact**, their average score under **Rating**, and when they sent it under **Submitted at**.
