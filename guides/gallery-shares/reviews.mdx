A review is feedback your client leaves on a gallery share. Turn reviews on to hear what they thought of the shoot, then read what they submit on the share.

## Allow reviews

A new share already lets clients leave a review after they look at the gallery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the gallery share">
    Open the gallery share. It opens on **Info**. See [Gallery shares](/guides/gallery-shares/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open review settings">
    Click **Review settings**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn reviews on or off">
    Leave **Allow reviews** on, or turn it off if your client should only view the photos. It saves right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can set the same switch while you create the link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the link">
    On the gallery, click **Share gallery**, then **Create link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Review">
    Click **Next** until you reach **Review**. **Allow reviews** is on here too.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the link">
    Click **Next**, then **Create link**. The share keeps the setting you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the questions

A new share asks every question. Clear the ones you don't need so the form fits the shoot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the questions">
    With **Allow reviews** on, look under **Review fields**. The questions stay hidden while reviews are off.
    {/* TODO screenshot: Review fields checkboxes under Allow reviews
    <Frame>
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/reviews-questions-1.png" alt="Review settings with Review fields and the question checkboxes" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/gallery-shares/reviews-questions-1-dark.png" alt="Review settings with Review fields and the question checkboxes" />
    </Frame>
    */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the questions you don't need">
    Clear the box next to any question you don't want to ask. The choices include **Overall experience**, **Communication**, **Photo session**, and **Price/quality**. You can also turn off **Photo quality**, **Title**, **Message**, or **Recommendation**. Each box saves right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you create a link, the same boxes are under **Allow reviews** on the **Review** step. Your choices save when you click **Create link**.

## Read a review

Once a client sends a review, click **Reviews** on the gallery share. The tab shows how many have come in.

Each row is one review. You see who sent it under **Contact**, their average score under **Rating**, and when they sent it under **Submitted at**.
