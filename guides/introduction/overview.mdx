Shootstack is where you organize a shoot and deliver it to your client. You upload photos, build galleries from them, and share them with a link. Your clients view the photos, pick favorites, and download them from the gallery site.

## Who it is for

Shootstack is for photographers and studios who deliver photos to clients, whether that's weddings, portraits, events, or commercial work. One workspace holds every shoot, contact, and gallery for your business.

## How a shoot moves through Shootstack

Start with a project for your shoot, then add photos and share a gallery with your client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    A project holds one shoot. Give it a name, a status, and a cover photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload photos into media folders">
    Inside the project, create a media folder and upload photos to it. Sort them by hand, or let Shootstack sort them automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a gallery">
    Pick which media folders the gallery shows and how it looks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the gallery">
    Create a gallery share to get a link for your client. Then choose whether they can favorite photos, download, or leave a review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your contacts, tasks, and notes live in the same workspace. Set your branding once and every gallery carries it.
