Find a photo by name, then open it full screen to see it up close and check its file info.

## Search photos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    In the media folder toolbar, click **Search photo...**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/view-search-1.webp" alt="Media folder toolbar with the Search photo... button" />

    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the file name">
    Start typing. Matching photos appear under **Photos** as you go.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/view-search-2.webp" alt="Search dialog with the Search photo... field and photos listed under Photos" />

    Click the photo to open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Press **/** in the folder to open search.
</Tip>

## Open a photo

Double-click a photo to open it full screen. You can also right-click the photo, or click **⋯** on its card. Then choose **Open**.

A single click selects the photo instead. See [Organize photos](/guides/media-folders/organize).

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/view-open-1.webp" alt="Photo card menu with Open, Quick view, and Copy filename" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/view-open-1-dark.webp" alt="Photo card menu with Open, Quick view, and Copy filename" />
</Frame>

In the viewer, use the arrow keys or **Previous photo** and **Next photo** to move through the folder. Click **Play slideshow** to show a new photo about every 5 seconds.

Double-click the photo to zoom in. You can also click **Zoom in** or **Zoom out**. Click **Reset** to reset the zoom, or **Close** to get back to the folder.

<Frame>
  <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/view-open-2.webp" alt="Full-screen photo with previous, next, slideshow, and zoom controls" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/view-open-2-dark.webp" alt="Full-screen photo with previous, next, slideshow, and zoom controls" />
</Frame>

Need the file name for an email or Lightroom? Right-click the photo, or click **⋯** on its card. Then click **Copy filename**.

## Quick view

Quick view enlarges a photo over the folder for a closer look, without opening the full viewer. Click a photo to select it, or move to one with the arrow keys, then press **Space**. You can also right-click it and choose **Quick view**. Click the photo, or the button at its top right, to close it.

## File info

In the viewer, click **Info** to open **File info** beside the photo. You'll see the original file and the web-optimized version Shootstack made from it.

If the photo has camera settings, they're under **Exposure triangle** and **Camera & lens**.
