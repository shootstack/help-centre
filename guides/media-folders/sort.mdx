The order of a media folder is the order your clients see. A new folder starts in **Manual sorting**, so you can drag photos into place, and new uploads land at the end.

## Sort photos by hand

Drag a photo by the handle at its top left and drop it where you want it. The handle only shows in a folder with **Manual sorting**.

## Sort by name, date, or random

Reorder the whole folder in one go, for example by name, then fine-tune by hand. The folder stays in **Manual sorting**, so you can still drag photos afterward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manual sorting">
    Click **Manual sorting** in the toolbar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/sort-name-1.png" alt="Manual sorting menu with Name, Date, and Random" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/sort-name-1-dark.png" alt="Manual sorting menu with Name, Date, and Random" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sort">
    Click **Name** or **Date**, then **Ascending** or **Descending**. Or click **Random** to shuffle the folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch to auto sorting

Auto sorting keeps the folder ordered by name or date without drag handles. It holds up to 2,500 photos, making room for larger shoots.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Auto sorting">
    Click **Manual sorting**, then **Auto sorting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the switch">
    In **Switch to auto sorting**, click **Continue**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/sort-auto-1.png" alt="Switch to auto sorting dialog with Continue and Cancel" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/sort-auto-1-dark.png" alt="Switch to auto sorting dialog with Continue and Cancel" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shootstack sorts the folder by name to start. To change the order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sorted by">
    Click the **Sorted by** button in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the order">
    Click **Name** or **Date**, then **Ascending** or **Descending**. The photos update right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch back to manual sorting

Switch back to place a few photos by hand, for example to open the folder with your best shot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Manual sorting">
    Click the **Sorted by** button, then **Manual sorting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Enable manual sorting**, click **Continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The drag handles come back, and the photos keep their current order as a starting point.

<Note>
A folder with **Manual sorting** holds up to 500 photos, and a folder with **Auto sorting** holds up to 2,500. With more than 500 photos, the folder stays in auto sorting until you move some out.
</Note>
