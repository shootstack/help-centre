Upload the photos from your shoot to a media folder to keep the set together.

## Upload photos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an upload">
    Click **Upload** at the right of the toolbar. Or, in an empty folder, click **Upload photos**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/upload-upload-1.webp" alt="Media folder toolbar with the Upload button on the right" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/upload-upload-1-dark.webp" alt="Media folder toolbar with the Upload button on the right" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your photos">
    Drag your photos onto the area that says **Drag and drop photos here to upload**, or click **Browse photos** to pick them from your computer.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/upload-upload-2.webp" alt="Upload photos screen with the drop area and Browse photos button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/upload-upload-2-dark.webp" alt="Upload photos screen with the drop area and Browse photos button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the upload finish">
    Each photo shows its progress. When they're all done, close the upload screen to get back to your folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you need to stop partway, click **Cancel upload**. If a few photos fail, click **Retry failed** to send them again.

## Skip or replace duplicates

A duplicate is a file with the same name as a photo already in this folder. Shootstack skips duplicates by default, so you can't upload a set twice by accident. Choose replace when you've re-edited photos and want the new versions to take over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open duplicate options">
    On the **Upload photos** screen, click **Skip duplicates**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/upload-duplicates-1.webp" alt="Upload photos header with the Skip duplicates menu open" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/upload-duplicates-1-dark.webp" alt="Upload photos header with the Skip duplicates menu open" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose skip or replace">
    Click **Skip** to keep the photos already in the folder, or **Replace** to overwrite them with the new files. Do this before you add the photos.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## File types and size

Shootstack accepts JPEG, PNG, and WebP files.

<Note>
Each file can be up to 100 MB.
</Note>

If a file can't be uploaded, Shootstack flags it and tells you why. Check whether its type is accepted and whether it's over 100 MB before trying again.

## When an upload is blocked

Sometimes the folder or your storage is full. Shootstack tells you on the **Upload photos** screen.

<Note>
A folder with **Manual sorting** holds up to 500 photos, and a folder with **Auto sorting** holds up to 2,500. For a large shoot, switch the folder to auto sorting or split the photos across folders. See [Sort photos](/guides/media-folders/sort).
</Note>

If your photo storage is full, free up space by deleting photos you no longer need, or upgrade your plan.
