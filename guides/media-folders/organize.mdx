As a shoot comes together, some photos end up in the wrong place: proofs that belong in the final folder, or outtakes you don't want clients to see. Select the photos, then copy, move, or delete them.

## Select photos

Click a photo to select it. Hold Command (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and click to add or remove individual photos. Hold Shift and click to select everything between two photos.

A bar appears with the count, such as **3 selected**, and the available actions. Click the count to clear the selection.

Right-click the photo, or click **⋯** on its card. You'll see the same actions for one photo.

<Tip>
Press **Command+A** or **Ctrl+A** in a folder with **Manual sorting** to select every photo.
</Tip>

## Copy photos

Copy when the same photos belong in two folders. The original folder stays as it is.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Copy to">
    With your photos selected, click **Copy to** on the bar. For one photo, right-click it and choose **Copy to**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-copy-1.webp" alt="Photo card menu with the Copy to option" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-copy-1-dark.webp" alt="Photo card menu with the Copy to option" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a folder">
    If the project has many folders, type in **Search folder...** first. In **Select a folder to copy to**, click the folder, and copying starts right away.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-copy-2.webp" alt="Select a folder to copy to dialog with Search folder and a list of media folders" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-copy-2-dark.webp" alt="Select a folder to copy to dialog with Search folder and a list of media folders" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can copy up to 500 photos at a time. For a bigger set, copy it in a few rounds.
</Note>

## Move photos

Move when photos are in the wrong folder and shouldn't stay behind.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Move to">
    With your photos selected, click **Move to** on the bar. For one photo, right-click it and choose **Move to**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-move-1.webp" alt="Photo card menu with the Move to option" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-move-1-dark.webp" alt="Photo card menu with the Move to option" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a folder">
    In **Select a folder to move to**, click the folder. The photos leave this folder as soon as the move finishes.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-move-2.webp" alt="Select a folder to move to dialog with Search folder and a list of media folders" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-move-2-dark.webp" alt="Select a folder to move to dialog with Search folder and a list of media folders" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can move up to 500 photos at a time. For a bigger set, move it in a few rounds.
</Note>

## Delete photos

<Warning>
Deleting photos removes them from Shootstack. This can't be undone. Keep the originals if you'll need them later.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    With your photos selected, click **Delete** on the bar. For one photo, right-click it and choose **Delete**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-delete-1.webp" alt="Photo card menu with the Delete option" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-delete-1-dark.webp" alt="Photo card menu with the Delete option" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete photo**, click **Delete**. For several photos, in **Delete selected photos**, click **Delete**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-delete-2.webp" alt="Delete photo dialog with the warning and the Delete button" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/media-folders/organize-delete-2-dark.webp" alt="Delete photo dialog with the warning and the Delete button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can delete up to 500 photos at a time. For a bigger set, delete it in a few rounds.
</Note>
