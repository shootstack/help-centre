Sometimes you need a photo back on your computer, for example to send one to a client or re-edit it. You can download the original you uploaded or the smaller web-optimized version Shootstack made from it.

## Download one photo

Right-click the photo, or click **⋯** on its card, then click **Download**. If the photo is already open in the viewer, click **Download** there.

<Note>
You can download one photo at a time. When you select several, you can copy, move, or delete them, but not download them.
</Note>

## Choose the file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a version">
    Under **Choose photo file**, keep **Web optimized photo file** for a smaller, web-ready version, or click **Original photo file** for the full-size file you uploaded. Each option shows its file name and size.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the photo">
    Click **Download photo**. On a phone, your share sheet opens so you can save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a download is blocked

Your plan includes a monthly amount of download bandwidth. Once it's used up, downloads pause until the next month starts.

<Note>
If the **Download photo** dialog says this month's bandwidth is used up, wait until next month or upgrade your plan to keep downloading.
</Note>
