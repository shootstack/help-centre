Choose how you hear about activity in Shootstack. Notification preferences let you switch email and in-app alerts on or off and use a weekday schedule.

## Choose how you receive notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/notifications-channels-1.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/notifications-channels-1-dark.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Click **Personal settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open notification preferences">
    Under **Personal** in the settings sidebar, click **Notifications**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/notifications-channels-2.webp" alt="Notifications settings page with the Personal sidebar group visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/notifications-channels-2-dark.webp" alt="Notifications settings page with the Personal sidebar group visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your notification channels">
    Turn **Email notifications** on or off to choose whether you receive email alerts. Use **In-app notifications** to choose whether you receive alerts in Shootstack. Changes save right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limit notifications to business hours

Turn on **Only receive notifications during business hours** on the same page.

<Note>
Business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Turn the switch off if you want to receive notifications outside those hours.
</Note>
