Choose a language you're comfortable reading in Shootstack. If you've moved or are working in another timezone, update your local settings here.

Changes save right away.

## Change your preferred language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/language-region-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/language-region-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Click **Personal settings**. Shootstack opens **General**, where you can change your language, country, and timezone.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/language-region-open-2.png" alt="General settings with language, country, and timezone controls visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/language-region-open-2-dark.png" alt="General settings with language, country, and timezone controls visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the language selector">
    Under **Language & region**, open **Preferred language**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your language">
    Choose English, Dutch, German, or French. Shootstack switches to your chosen language.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose your country

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the country selector">
    Under **Language & region**, open **Country**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your country">
    Choose your country from the list, using the search box if you need to find it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing a country doesn't change your timezone. Set it separately below.

## Set your timezone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the timezone selector">
    On **General**, open **Timezone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your timezone">
    Choose your timezone from the list, or use the search box to find it. Timezones for your country appear first, with the full list under **Other**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
