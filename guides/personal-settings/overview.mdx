Personal settings let you choose how you work in Shootstack, from the language you read to the notifications you receive.

## Open personal settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/overview-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/overview-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Click **Personal settings**. Shootstack opens **General** with your personal details.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/overview-open-2.png" alt="Personal settings page with General selected and profile details visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/overview-open-2-dark.png" alt="Personal settings page with General selected and profile details visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find the setting you need

Choose a page under **Personal** in the settings sidebar:

- **General**: [update your profile](/guides/personal-settings/profile) or [set your language and region](/guides/personal-settings/language-region).
- **Appearance**: [choose the app's theme and color](/guides/personal-settings/appearance).
- **Notifications**: [choose how you receive notifications](/guides/personal-settings/notifications).

Your profile, language, country, and timezone settings are saved for your account. Appearance is saved in the browser you're using.

## Return to your workspace

Click **Settings** beside the back arrow at the top of the sidebar to return to your workspace's home page.
