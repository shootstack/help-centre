Choose a light or dark app theme and an accent color while you work in Shootstack. Your choices save in the browser you're using.

## Choose a theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/appearance-theme-1.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/appearance-theme-1-dark.webp" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Click **Personal settings**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/appearance-theme-2.webp" alt="Personal settings sidebar with Appearance highlighted under Personal" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/appearance-theme-2-dark.webp" alt="Personal settings sidebar with Appearance highlighted under Personal" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open appearance settings">
    Under **Personal**, click **Appearance**.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/appearance-theme-3.webp" alt="Appearance page with Theme choices visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/appearance-theme-3-dark.webp" alt="Appearance page with Theme choices visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your theme">
    Under **Theme**, pick the appearance you want:

    - **Light** for a light app appearance
    - **Dark** for a dark app appearance
    - **System** to use your device's light or dark appearance

    The app applies your choice right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a color

On **Appearance**, choose one of the six accent colors under **Color**. The app applies your choice right away.
