Update your account name when your details change, or add a recent profile photo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the app sidebar.

    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/profile-open-1.png" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/profile-open-1-dark.png" alt="Workspace menu with Personal settings highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Click **Personal settings**. Shootstack opens **General** with your personal details.
    <Frame>
      <img className="dark:hidden block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/profile-open-2.png" alt="General settings with the profile photo and name fields visible" />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/images/guides/personal-settings/profile-open-2-dark.png" alt="General settings with the profile photo and name fields visible" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're already in settings, click **General** under **Personal**.

## Change your name

In **First name** or **Last name**, type over the current name. Changes save after you stop typing.

## Upload or replace your profile photo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Click **Upload photo**. Use the same button to replace your current photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a photo">
    Choose a JPG/JPEG, PNG, or WEBP file from your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the crop">
    In **Crop photo**, drag the image to position it and adjust **Zoom** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the photo">
    Click **Apply**. The dialog closes and your new profile photo appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Cancel** in the crop dialog to keep your current photo.

<Note>
The original photo can be up to 10 MB. For a larger file, export a smaller copy before uploading.
</Note>

## Remove your profile photo

<Warning>
Deleting your profile photo removes it from your account. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove the photo">
    Click **Remove photo** beside your current photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete user profile photo**, click **Delete**. Your initials replace the photo.
  </Step>
</Steps>
