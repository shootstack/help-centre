---
title: Why didn't my client receive my email
description: The email landed in spam, the address was wrong, or sending is paused.
---

Start with your client's spam folder and email address. If the email is still missing, check its status in the email history.

## The email landed in spam or promotions

Ask your client to check spam and promotions for an email from your workspace name.

## The email address has a typo

Open the email history and check the address. If it's wrong, ask your client for the right one and update the contact. Then send the gallery again.

</Card>

## The status shows Blocked

The address can't receive mail, or your client marked an earlier email as spam, so Shootstack stops sending to it. Ask your client for another address.

## The status shows Bounced

Your client's email provider sent the email back. This can happen for a temporary reason, such as a full inbox.

Send the gallery again later. If the next email shows Blocked, ask your client for another address.

<Card title="Send a gallery share email" icon="/assets/icons/mail-01.svg" href="/guides/emails/send">
  Send the gallery link and a message to your client.
</Card>

## The status doesn't confirm delivery yet

Waiting and Sent don't confirm delivery.

Delayed means there's a temporary delivery problem, such as a full inbox. Shootstack is still trying to deliver the email, so check the status again later.

<Card title="Check email activity" icon="/assets/icons/mail-01.svg" href="/guides/emails/activity">
  See whether an email arrived and what happened next.
</Card>

## Email sending is paused for the workspace

After repeated spam complaints, Shootstack pauses sending for the workspace and **Email sending paused** appears when you try to send. Open **Help**, then click **Chat with support** to restore access.

If none of these fit, open **Help**, then click **Chat with support**.
