---
title: Why didn't my client receive my email
description: The email landed in spam, the address was wrong, or sending is paused.
---

<Accordion title="The email landed in spam or promotions">
  Ask your client to check spam and promotions for an email from your workspace name. To stop it happening again, see [Troubleshoot email delivery](/guides/emails/deliverability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The email address has a typo">
  Open the email history and check the address. If it's wrong, ask your client for the right one and update the contact. Then send the gallery again. See [Troubleshoot email delivery](/guides/emails/deliverability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The status shows Blocked">
  The address can't receive mail, or your client marked an earlier email as spam, so Shootstack stops sending to it. Ask your client for another address. See [Troubleshoot email delivery](/guides/emails/deliverability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The status shows Bounced">
  Your client's email provider sent the email back, sometimes for a temporary reason such as a full inbox. Send the gallery again later. If the next email shows Blocked, ask your client for another address. See [Send a gallery share email](/guides/emails/send).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The status doesn't confirm delivery yet">
  Waiting and Sent don't confirm delivery. Delayed means delivery hit a temporary problem, such as a full inbox, and delivery is still being tried. Check the status again later. See [Check email activity](/guides/emails/activity).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Email sending is paused for the workspace">
  After repeated spam complaints, Shootstack pauses sending for the workspace and **Email sending paused** appears when you try to send. Open **Help** and click **Chat with support** to restore access.
</Accordion>
