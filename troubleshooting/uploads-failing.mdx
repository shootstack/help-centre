---
title: Why are my uploads failing?
description: Uploads can fail because of browser issues, an interrupted connection, file limits, or full storage.
---

Once the current batch finishes, click **Retry failed** on the **Upload photos** screen to try any failed uploads again. If they still fail, check your browser and connection first.

## Your browser needs an update or refresh

Update your browser to the latest version, then refresh Shootstack. Safari updates are included with macOS updates.

If a normal refresh doesn't help, try a hard refresh. This reloads Shootstack without using saved browser files.

| Browser | Hard refresh shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on Windows | `Ctrl + Shift + R` |
| Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on Mac | `Command + Shift + R` |
| Safari on Mac | `Option + Command + R` |

Refreshing clears the upload queue. After refreshing, open **Skip duplicates** and choose **Replace** before adding only the photos that didn't finish. Otherwise, Shootstack may skip them because their filenames are already in the folder.

<Card title="Upload photos" icon="/assets/icons/image-upload.svg" href="/guides/media-folders/upload">
  Upload your photos and choose how to handle files with the same names.
</Card>

## Your internet connection is interrupted

An unstable connection can interrupt an upload before it finishes. Reconnect or try a more stable network, then click **Retry failed**. Keep the browser tab open until the upload finishes.

## The file type or size isn't supported

Shootstack accepts JPEG, PNG, and WebP files. Export other formats, such as RAW or HEIC, in a supported format before uploading.

<Note>
Each file can be up to 100 MB. Export larger photos as smaller files before trying again.
</Note>

## The photo can't be processed

A file can upload but still fail when Shootstack tries to process it. If a photo shows "Couldn't process photo", export a fresh copy from your photo editor and upload it again with **Replace** selected.

<Note>
Photos can be up to 15,000 pixels on each side, export a smaller image if it exceeds the limit.
</Note>

## The media folder is full

<Note>
A folder with **Manual sorting** holds up to 500 photos, and a folder with **Auto sorting** holds up to 2,500.
</Note>

Switch a full manually sorted folder to auto sorting to make more room, or split the photos across media folders.

<Card title="Sort photos" icon="/assets/icons/sorting-05.svg" href="/guides/media-folders/sort">
  Switch between manual and auto sorting for your media folder.
</Card>

## Your photo storage is full

If your photo storage is full, free up space by deleting photos you no longer need, or upgrade your plan.

  Check your photo storage usage and plan allowance.
</Card>
