title: Why is the image quality in my gallery lower than the original?
description: Galleries show smaller web versions of your photos so clients download less data, while your originals keep their full quality.
---

Shootstack creates a smaller, web-optimized copy of each photo for your gallery, so your clients don't download full-size files as they browse.

## Original photos and web versions

The original is the file you uploaded, with its full resolution and quality. Shootstack keeps it unchanged.

The web version is a separate copy, resized and compressed for viewing on a screen. It can show less detail than the original, especially when you zoom in.

## Why galleries use web versions

Original photos are often large files. The larger the files and the more photos a gallery contains, the more data your clients have to download as they browse.

With smaller web versions, each photo your client opens is less data to download.

Large photos also use more of the device's memory. Loading many at once can make a gallery slow or unresponsive. If the browser runs out of memory, the gallery can stop working.
