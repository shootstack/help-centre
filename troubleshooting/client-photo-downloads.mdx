---
title: Why can't my client download their photos?
description: Downloads can be blocked by watermarks, gallery share settings, or usage limits.
---

Start by checking the gallery's media folders and the gallery share's download settings. If those allow downloads, check your bandwidth usage and your client's browser.

## The gallery only contains watermarked media folders

Clients can view watermarked photos, but can't download them. Add a media folder without a watermark to the gallery for the photos your client should download.

<Card title="Media folders" icon="/assets/icons/folder-01.svg" href="/guides/media-folders/overview">
  Create a media folder without a watermark for your final photos.
</Card>

## No media folders have been added to the gallery

Uploading photos to a media folder doesn't add them to a gallery automatically. Add the folders that contain the photos your client should download.

<Card title="Add media folders" icon="/assets/icons/folder-01.svg" href="/guides/galleries/folders">
  Choose which media folders appear in your gallery.
</Card>

## Downloads are turned off for the gallery share

Gallery downloads and individual photo downloads have separate settings. Turn on the download option your client needs in the gallery share.

<Card title="Allow downloads" icon="/assets/icons/sent.svg" href="/guides/gallery-shares/downloads">
  Choose what your clients can download and change the gallery share's download limits.
</Card>

## The download limit has been reached

Raise the gallery or photo download limit in the gallery share's download settings.

If it's already at the highest allowed limit, create a new gallery share and send your client the new link.

<Card title="Gallery shares" icon="/assets/icons/sent.svg" href="/guides/gallery-shares/overview">
  Create a new gallery share and send its link to your client.
</Card>

## Your workspace's monthly download bandwidth is used up

Downloads pause when your workspace uses up its monthly download bandwidth. Wait until the next month starts, or upgrade to a plan with more bandwidth.

<Card title="Check workspace usage" icon="/assets/icons/settings-02.svg" href="/guides/workspace-settings/usage">
  Check your download bandwidth usage and plan allowance.
</Card>

## The gallery is open inside Facebook or Instagram

A gallery link opened inside another app can use the app's built-in browser, which can cause download problems. Ask your client to copy the gallery link and open it directly in Safari or Chrome, then try downloading again.
