---
title: Can watermarks be removed from my photos?
description: Watermarks identify proofs and block client downloads in Shootstack, but AI and photo editors can remove them.
---

AI tools and photo editors can remove a watermark from a copy of a photo. Watermarks still help with proofing, especially with clear terms your client has agreed to.

## How watermarks help with proofing

A watermark gives your client a visible reminder that the photos are for review and selection only. Shootstack also keeps photos in watermarked media folders out of client downloads, even when downloads are enabled for the gallery share. Your client can still view the proofs and choose favorites if favorites are enabled.

## What about screenshots?

Even with downloads blocked, someone can take a screenshot or save a copy of a preview they can see. A watermark can't prevent this, so your agreement with your client matters too: it sets clear boundaries for how the proofs can be used.

## Set clear expectations with your clients

Start the conversation when you discuss the job, so your client knows from the beginning that the proofs are for review and selection only. Your terms should spell this out in a proofing section: clients can't download or screenshot proofs, share or publish them, edit them, remove watermarks, or use them in AI tools.

Have your client agree to the terms when accepting the job, before work begins. When you send the gallery link later, include a short reminder of the proofing restrictions, so it reinforces what you've already agreed.

If none of these fit, open **Help**, then click **Chat with support**.
